B.Tech Admission — Application Process

Students must complete the online application form through the designated admission portal.

The application process includes:

1. Registering on the admission portal.
2. Entering personal and academic information.
3. Uploading the required documents.
4. Reviewing the application.
5. Submitting the application.
6. Completing the applicable fee payment.

Students should verify all information before final submission.

After submission, students should keep their application or registration number for future reference.
