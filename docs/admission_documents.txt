B.Tech Admission — Required Documents

Students applying for B.Tech admission must submit the following documents:

1. Government-issued identity proof.
2. Recent passport-size photograph.
3. Class 10 marksheet.
4. Class 12 marksheet.
5. Transfer certificate, if applicable.
6. Migration certificate, if applicable.
7. Caste certificate, if applicable.
8. Income certificate, if applying for an income-based scholarship.
9. Domicile certificate, if required by the admission category.

All documents should be clear and readable. Students should verify the latest admission notice for the final list of required documents.

For technical problems while submitting documents through the online admission portal, students should contact the college admission support team.
